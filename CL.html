DOMINICAN ADVOCACY MESSAGE

Truth We Share, Love is Everywhere

We the group 4, as students it can be hard and confusing who we want to be in the future and how to handle different task in our school and outside our school which is our Reality.For us living the Dominican way means we are building a life with faith and living with values through our daily life. Our group made this Advocacy Message to prove that trust in ourselves is built and faith makes us stronger.

For our Advocacy Message we thoughtfully chose 4 good as we are also the group 4 habits:

    1. Our Prayer Prayer helps us to take a break and have deep talks with God. Praying means we know god is with us and we trust him with all our hearts. Even a few quiet moments of prayer can bring us peace and guide us to the right choices so we know our directions.

    2. Our Unity We want a classroom and a community to feel like a home for everyone to feel safe and loved. A classroom and a Community filled with love and respect towards each other’s differences. Making sure everyone is included and mistakes are open to improvements.

    3. Our Service and Love Service and Love means showing up and matching our words into our actions. It means helping those whose in need with genuine intentions and without expecting something in return. Having the Opportunity to Provide Care and Support to people is already a blessing itself.

    4. Our Faith Faith gives us courage when we feel down and weak. It reminds us to do our best and take like easy at the same time. Through small and right choices everyday we can improve our faith.

GROUP 4’s WAYS OF SPREADING AND TEACHING THE WORD OF GOD As Dominicans, we can spread the word of God with simple actions every day. We do not need to be perfect or make a big speech, Because small steps along the way can make the biggest changes if we value and love what we do.

We can start with the people around us. We, the Group 4 Cannot take actions or teach if we dont apply it first on ourselves so we will still learn and teach at the same time to grow even more.

    1. A helping hand to those in need inside the classrooms A student notices their quiet classmate struggling with their activities so she offer to help and she said that she is not left behind and she is always there whenever the quiet classmate is in need.

    2. Make an Advocacy group A group could leave encouraging notes for teachers, classmates and the community. give food to those in hunger and help up to clean the environment.

    3. Using social media the right way We could post quotes and bible verses online to share motivational and positive words. this encourage people to make more time on God or stories about how faith helps them through difficult situations.

    4. Showing kindness and forgiveness Instead of resenting people who did us wrong, we should normalize forgiveness, because all of us makes mistakes and that is because we are simply just humans, we are not perfect. we should just learn from our mistakes and improve ourselves so we dont make the same mistake twice. Forgiving them is a example pf catholic teaching.

    5.Inviting someone or someone we know to church If a friend or someone you happen to know is curious about Christianity, you could invite them to a youth group or church. We could go to church at the same time spread the teachings of God. We aim to serve our people and community through good actions.
