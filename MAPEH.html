Campaign Name: Eco-Safety

What is it all about?-We created a jingle and It includes our own lyrics but the song is from an artist and we just changed and composed the lyrics.sung with our real voices, Instruments from the original song, simple dance steps, and meaningful props. This video is the MAPEH part of our community campaign website.

PROPS

    Colorful shirts- Bright Future. if we follow the basic rules and take good care of our environment we will have a colorful and a bright future ahead.

    Blue and Brown shirts- Blue represents- water and healthy rules while Brown represent- earth and soil

    Caps- to keep our head covered from dusts

    Handkerchiefs - to help clean the surface

    Facemask- protects you and others from breathing in harmful germs, dust, smoke, and chemical fumes.

    Gloves -protect your hands from dirt, germs, and harmful chemicals so you stay safe and do not spread waste or chemicals.

    Broom-Cleans and sweeps away trash, dirt, and waste so our surroundings stay clean and free from pollution.

    Dustpan-Collects swept trash and waste properly so nothing stays scattered and we can dispose of it the right way.

    Chemicals- example of chemicals

    Headbands with Quotes -We put quotes on headbands about environmental issues and chemical safety rules

    Pompoms- additional props only

​Every prop chosen for the Eco-Safety campaign serves a clear purpose.

The colorful shirts, blue and brown outfits, and quote headbands visually remind our community of the bright future we can build when we respect our soil and water.

Meanwhile, the Useful items such as the face masks, gloves, brooms, dustpans, caps, and handkerchiefs shows the real, everyday safety steps needed when handling waste and chemicals.

​By combining these meaningful props with simple dance steps and our custom jingle lyrics, this MAPEH video brings our campaign message to life in an engaging, easy to understand way. Small actions, when done with the right safety knowledge, lead to big changes. Together, through awareness and proper safety habits, we can keep our surroundings clean, safe, for everyone
