The Eco-Squad Warriors

Topic- Improper Waste Disposal

Theme-Small actions create lasting environmental change through community cooperation.

CHARACTERS

Daizel- narrator

Shalanie- The environmental issue, chemical safety educated student and a advocate student

Yuan- The "i dont care" student

Carl- the confused and curious but willing to learn student

Ghio- The lazy student

Sheen- The student who throw her trust anywhere on streets

Shakira- The responsible student who knows how to segregate her trash the right way ( we used our own names on our script)

Scene 1

– Introduction (Visual Cue- School hallway trashed with empty milk tea cups, banana peels, plastic wrappers and many other trash around the school.) (Audio Cue- Let Down- Remastered by Radiohead)

Daizel (Narrator - Standing on a stage with microphone) Welcome to our school! As your narrator today, let me tell you a story about how everyday habits affect our planet. Look at this hallway it looks like a tornado made of plastic just hit our campus!

Shalanie- Look at this mess! As an environmental advocate, Ofcourse I can't stay silent. Our school environment needs serious environmental protection right now before things get worse!

Sheen-(Takes a dramatic sip of milk tea, drops the plastic cup right on the floor) Oops! My hands suddenly lost all power. Trash goes to the ground, right? It’s way easier than walking!

Ghio-(Yawning, stretching lazily on a nearby bench) Walking five steps to the trash bin requires way too much energy. Nature created the earth, so nature will handle the trash. It’s natural selection! duhh?

Yuan- Whatever. Trash on the ground is not my problem. The janitors get paid, right? Why should I waste my precious time caring about a little plastic?

Scene 2

– Conflict (Visual Cue- Carl walks in , steps directly on Sheen's banana peel, slips with arms doing flying actions and tumbles onto the bench next to Ghio.) (Audio Cue- Loud slide whistle followed by a Cricket sound)

Daizel-(Narrator) And just like that... carelessness meets consequence!

Carl- Woah! Ouch! Why is there a banana peel on the hallway floor? Is someone trying to set a trap for me?!

Shalanie- That’s a hazard caused by improper waste disposal! And it’s not just banana peels look over there by the garden! Someone left open bottles of bleach and expired cleaning liquids just lying around.

Ghio-(Grins proudly) Ha! Good thing I poured those old liquid chemicals right into the soil behind the school garden last week! The dirt was looking messy, so I thought the bleach would help clean the dirt! like an ex Out of sight, out of mind! haha! (Text on Screen- Dramatic Irony-Pouring liquid chemicals and bleach into the ground destroys plant life and poisons our drinking water!)

Carl-Wait a second... clean the dirt?! It's dirt! Doesn't pouring random liquid chemicals poison the plants? I thought the plants would just drink it and grow faster!

Shalanie- No, Carl! As a student advocate, it is my job to educate everyone. When toxic liquids goes into the earth, dangerous chemical reactions happen. They ruin our soil, poison the water, and destroy our clean surroundings! Dumping liquid hazardous waste like that violates basic chemical safety.

Scene 3

– Realization (Audio Cue -"Heal the World" by Michael Jackson, Calm and soft Piano instrumental)

Daizel: (Narrator) While everyone was busy arguing, one student took a step back to reflect on what truly matters.

Shakira-(Soliloquy - Steps forward into a spotlight while the rest of the cast freezes in place) (To herself, holding a plastic bottle) Is it really that hard to care? Every single day, people throw things away without thinking about where the right "away" actually is. If we ignore our dirt and trash today, our entire community suffers tomorrow. We cannot keep waiting for someone else to clean up our mess. Responsibility doesn't start with the school or the government it starts right here with me. (Visual Cue-Spotlight slowly back to normal lighting.

Shakira pulls out three color-coded trash bags.) Shakira- Look, everyone! Green is for biodegradable food waste, blue is for recyclable plastic, and red is for hazardous materials like toxic liquid cleaners and empty spray cans. This is proper waste segregation!

Sheen- (Monologue - Stepping forward with an embarrassed and sad expression) You know... I always thought throwing garbage anywhere made me look cool and carefree, like I was too busy to care. But seeing Carl almost break his back on my banana peel made me realize something. My dirty habit isn't cool at all it actually hurts the people around me. I don't want to be the reason someone gets hurt or our school gets ruined. I want to change.

Scene 4

– Resolution (Audio Cue- “Takas” by Freddy Corpuz Instrumental)

Daizel- (Narrator) Realization turns into action! The team starts to see the bigger picture.

Shalanie- Admitting it is the first step, Sheen! As environmental advocates, we must remember that when spilled bleach and old liquid chemicals contaminate our regular waste, they create toxics and put our school janitors in serious danger.

Carl- I get it now! So if we practice Bayanihan which means working together as a team to keep our spaces clean we can protect both our health and our natural resources! I maybe slow and half as a sloth but learning basic rules makes me feel good!

Ghio- Fine... I guess I shouldn't try to "clean" the dirt anymore. Standing up to put this plastic bottle in the blue recycling bin burns three calories anyway. Consider it my workout for the day, I’m in!

Yuan- Well... if even Ghio is getting off the bench, I guess I really have no excuse left. Give me a trash bag. Let’s pick this up together. (Props- All characters grab brooms, color-coded recycling bins, and reusable trash bags, cleaning the hallway together with energetic moves.)

Scene 5

– Campaign Message (Visual Cue-All seven characters step forward, with Daizel joining the group line on stage, holding two large campaign banner, that say "Eco-Friendly" and "Eco- safety Warriors")

Daizel-(Narrator & Character) Protecting our environment isn't a one person job it’s our shared duty to push for real climate action!

Shalanie-Let's stand up for our planet and practice household chemical safety!

Shakira-Separate your trash correctly!

Daizel- Preserve our community for the future!

All Characters:

(In unison, taking a step forward) "Small Actions Makes Big Difference" (Audio Cue- "What Makes You Beautiful" Instrumental) (Text on Screen-Eco-Squad Warrior: Campaign- Waste Right, Earth Bright and Bayanihan Shines Bright! )Ending Audio cue -(Audio Cue- "What Makes You Beautiful" composed into environmental issues and chemical safety lyrics. happy, starts soft then loud.
